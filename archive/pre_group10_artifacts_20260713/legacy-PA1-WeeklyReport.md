# GroupID-PA1 Weekly Scrum Meeting Minutes

## Section 1: Process Overview

Process methodology: RUP + Scrum.

RUP phases applied in PA1:

- Inception: form the group, choose the topic, and choose FIFA.com and Chess.com as the two products.

- Elaboration: analyze users, contexts, HCI concepts, risks, and screen/source evidence.

- Construction: write ProductResearch, PotentialSolutions, PeerReview, and WeeklyReport.

- Transition: export PDFs, validate the ZIP, and prepare the Moodle submission.

Scrum process:

- One sprint is fixed for 2 weeks, corresponding with PA1.

- Sprint 1 contains four meetings: Sprint Planning, Weekly Scrum 1, Weekly Scrum 2, and Sprint Review and Retrospective.

Templates used:

- Weekly scrum meeting minutes template.

- Sprint planning meeting minutes template.

- Sprint review meeting minutes template.

## Section 8: Sprint Review and Retrospective Meeting Minutes

### =========== 22/06, Sprint 1, Sprint Review and Retrospective ===============

- Date: 2026-06-22.

- Time: 20:00 to 20:50.

- Team members present: Le Minh, Nguyen Vu Bach, Pham Nguyen Gia Bao, Trang Minh Nhut.

- Team members absent: None.

- Meeting objective: inspect the PA1 increment, review process outcomes, agree corrective actions, and decide the final draft packaging state.

### What went well

The product pair, ownership, official-source protocol, personas, ten detailed use cases, annotated evidence, and balanced workload were completed consistently.

### What went wrong

The first WeeklyReport was not in the strict process template, and one PotentialSolutions evidence table drifted from the canonical drawback meanings.

### What problems occurred

Some dynamic/login-only states could not be captured; report tables became dense; GroupID and real classroom feedback were unavailable.

### What caused the problems

The generator had duplicated mappings and hard-coded readiness assumptions, and validation checked ID presence rather than semantic relationships.

### What the team will do differently in the next sprint

Centralize configuration and canonical mappings first, validate one vertical slice before duplication, and collect real feedback immediately after presentation.

### Lessons learned

Visual polish cannot compensate for weak traceability or missing process evidence; final readiness must be gated by real identifiers, authentic feedback, extracted PDF text, and exact package contents.

### Final PA1 packaging decision

Generate a READY DRAFT package with exactly four PDFs in GroupID-PA1.zip. Do not claim READY FINAL while GroupID or real peer feedback remains pending.

### Actions

Le Minh runs the build and validators; Nguyen Vu Bach and Pham Nguyen Gia Bao review product evidence; Trang Minh Nhut rechecks canonical traceability; the team fills real feedback and group ID before final mode.

### Summary of the meeting

The increment is acceptable as a validated draft after strict fixes, with two explicit external-information blockers remaining for final submission.
