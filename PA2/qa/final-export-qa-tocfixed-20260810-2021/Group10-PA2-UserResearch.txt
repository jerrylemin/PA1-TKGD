progressive disclosure, user control, and error prevention organize the PA2

2. Tasks and protocol
2.1 FIFA task set
1. Find ticket entry.
2. Compare tournament status.
3. Decide buy, wait, register interest, resale, or hospitality.
4. Identify destination before leaving FIFA.com.
5. Resume on mobile.
6. Recover from missing or stale status.
2.2 Chess task set
1. Choose a beginner route.
2. Continue a lesson.
3. Solve a puzzle.
4. Open analysis or review.
5. Identify one important mistake.
6. Try a better move.
7. Read a plain-language explanation.
8. Continue to practice.
9. Reveal advanced detail on demand.

5.1 Pattern Summary
FIFA patterns center on ticket-state clarity, freshness, mobile context, and
partner handoff trust. Chess patterns center on entry choice, beginner
explanation, and a clear continuation into practice.
5.2 Result Summary
The simulated sessions support two PA3 hypotheses: a FIFA decision layer
should keep state, freshness, route, and recovery together; a Chess beginner
review should show one interpretable mistake and one next practice action
before advanced analysis.
