2.2 F-UC02 - Compare ticket states
Field Record
ID F-UC02
Goal Compare ticket states
Scope FIFA Ticket Confidence Module
Primary actor Ticket planner
Supporting actor Governed ticket-state feed
Trigger A tournament context is selected.
Context is valid; state feed may return current, stale,
Preconditions missing, sold out, resale, waiting room, or hospitality
states.
Success Available states are compared without treating missing
guarantee data as an outcome.
Priority Critical
Frequency Every planning visit
Main flow
1. Request states for the selected context.
2. Display each channel with a text state label.
3. Compare primary sale, resale, hospitality, wait, and interest options.
4. Explain unavailable and sold-out distinctions.
5. Choose a state to inspect or stay on the comparison.
Alternate flows
A1 at step 2: State is stale; retain it with timestamp and warning.
A2 at step 2: State is missing; show unknown and retry, not sold out.
A3 at step 3: Waiting room is active; explain temporary queue behavior.
Exception flows
E1: Feed conflict shows both governed sources and blocks a confident
action.
E2: Network loss retains the last labeled snapshot.
Field Record
Sold out, resale, waiting room, missing, and stale are
Business rules
mutually explicit labels.
