6. Weighted prioritization
Formula: Weighted score = Severity x 25% + Task frequency x 15% +
Risk x 20% + Reach x 10% + Evidence strength x 15% + PA3 relevance x
15%. Each factor is scored 1-5; maximum is 5.00.

8.3 Final Rationale
Final rationale: FIFA status and freshness remains the highest-priority
problem because it combines high severity, trust risk, repeated planning use,
and direct PA3 testability. Chess beginner review remains the selected
problem because explanation and practice continuation jointly determine
whether review becomes learning.
