1. Input evidence and analysis method
Inputs are the validated screenshot index, PA1 canonical drawbacks, the User Research task analysis, and PA2 requirements. Notes are explicitly labeled [OBS], [PA1], or [IDEA] so observation is not confused with inherited evidence or inference.
2. Brainstorming protocol
Diverge from each focal task and evidence item into concise problem notes.
Add solution ideas without judging feasibility.
Cluster by task breakdown, not by product page name.
Normalize duplicates and reject notes based only on misleading filenames.
Score candidate problems with a transparent rubric, then select two tough problems.

6. Cluster interpretation
FIFA-A3 and FIFA-A4 converge on a single trust-sensitive decision: understand status and destination before leaving FIFA.com.
CHESS-A4 and CHESS-A5 converge on next-action priority: a beginner needs one interpretable learning/review action before advanced depth.
Mobile and promotional findings amplify both problems but are not selected as independent tough problems.
Premove and Focus Mode remain PA1 findings only; the PA2 screenshot corpus does not validate those states.
