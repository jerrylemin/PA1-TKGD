1. Executive summary
This study continues Group10's PA1 comparison of FIFA.com as a browse-first official football ecosystem and Chess.com as an action-first play-and-learning platform. It combines source analysis with a structured try-it-yourself artifact walkthrough, as permitted by the PA2 assignment (PA2-LKDuy-2026-Public.pdf, p. 2).
2. PA2 research objectives
Describe users, goals, contexts, capabilities, constraints, artifacts, and task breakdowns without inventing participant data.
Validate PA1 findings against current visible interface states.
Identify two evidence-supported tough problems suitable for three alternative concepts each in PA3.
Preserve canonical PA1 persona and drawback identifiers.

4. Research questions
RQ1 Who performs each focal task, in what setting, and with what success condition?
RQ2 Which domain and application experience changes the user's interpretation of controls?
RQ3 Which device and attention constraints change task entry, scanning, and continuation?
RQ4 What information and artifacts are required at each decision point?
RQ5 Where do trust, error risk, or cross-property boundaries appear?
RQ6 What breakdowns and workarounds are supported by visible interface states?

13. Cross-product insights
Both products expose recognizable domain artifacts, but the decision burden differs: FIFA asks users to verify information and boundaries; Chess.com asks users to prioritize actions and interpret depth.
Mobile layouts preserve major tasks through stacking, while increasing vertical continuation and reducing simultaneous overview.
A positive baseline exists in Chess.com's Learn-to-Play path: a visible Next Lesson action demonstrates how one clear continuation can coexist with a rich product.
The selected problems are TP-FIFA ticket confidence and TP-CHESS beginner learning/review priority.
14. Limitations
No external participant behavior, interviews, surveys, quotes, or measured usability outcomes.
Screenshots are time-bound captures and cannot establish universal production behavior.
Requested lecture PDFs LN01-LN04 are absent; method citations use the PA2 assignment and PA1 continuity.
Filename labels were rejected wherever pixels did not support the state.
Future work requires interviews or direct observation with representative users.
