Report overview and evidence protocol   
 
Summary 
This report compares FIFA.com and Chess.com as web experiences with opposite interaction centers. FIFA.com is browse -first: users browse, compare, follow, verify, and hand off to FIFA+ for 
video.  
Chess.com is action -first: users play, review, analyze, train, and enter competitive flows.

FIFA.com Browse-first football web ecosystem for official news, match -following, rankings, tournament discovery, ticketing trust, and FIFA+ watch handoff.  
Chess.com Action-first chess web platform for games, review, self-analysis, puzzles, lessons, study plans, tournaments, and fair -play-guided competitive play.

FIFA.com observed web captures   
 
Figure F-01. FIFA.com home hierarchy 
 
Analysis 
• Related persona/task: F -P1 casual fan enters FIFA.com during a short break to find matches and headlines.  
• What the image shows: hero content, countdown, match strip, and article blocks compete for first -view attention.  
• HCI reading: visual hierarchy splits attention between promotional content and task entry.  
• Impact: low -attention users may scroll or choose the wrong ro ute before Match Centre.  
• Design implication: keep common tasks such as Scores, News, Rankings, Tickets, and Watch visible near the 
first view.  
Figure F-02. FIFA.com desktop navigation 
 
Analysis 
• Related persona/task: F -P1 and F-P2 use top navigation for Match Centre, News, Rankings, and Tickets.  
• What the image shows: key official labels remain visible in the header.  
• HCI reading: Recognition over recall helps users choose known task labels instead of remembering URLs.  
• Benefit: faster first step for schedule, ranking, ticket, and news tasks.  
• Limit: More and sibling properties still expand the navigation map.

Analysis 
• Related persona/task: F -P1 uses mobile in public transport or campus contexts with interrupted attention.

Figure F-04. FIFA.com Match Centre 
 
Analysis 
• Related persona/task: F -UC1 and F -UC2, checking today fixtures, live scores, or results.  
• What the image shows: date rail, search, live toggle, match rows, sort, and filter controls.  
• HCI reading: Visibility of system status and date -based mental model support fixture scanning.  
• Benefit: users can anchor the task around day, competition, and live state.  
• Risk: sparse or slow client -rendered rows still create waiting and retry behavior.

Comparison  
 
Dimension  FIFA.com  Chess.com  
Task type  Browse, compare, follow, verify, then hand off to FIFA+ for video.  Do, review, analyze, train, and enter competitive flows.  
Target users  Fans checking official football information, tickets, rankings, and video.  Players who want fast games, feedback, training, and fair competitive play.  
Information scent  Strong for official labels; weaker when tasks cross properties.  Strong action labels o n homepage and support flows.  
Learnability Easy for news/rankings; ticket and watch handoff need clearer status.  Easy to start; analysis and training depth require guidance.  
Cognitive load  Browse surfaces and rails can become dense.  Feature breadth and a nalysis controls can overwhelm novices.  
Error risk Wrong ticket source or lost context during handoff.  Queued premove, hidden Focus Mode, and access -limit interruptions.  
Best next solution  Task-first navigation, handoff explainer, ticket dashboard, article utility rail.  Goal-based onboarding, beginner analysis preset, premove preview, Focus coachmark.
