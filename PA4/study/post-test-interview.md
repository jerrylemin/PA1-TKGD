# PA4 post-test interview guide

Ask these questions after both flows. Use neutral follow-up prompts such as “What made you say that?” or “Can you point to the part you mean?” Do not suggest that an answer is correct.

Create at least one timestamped, non-empty feedback record for every verified participant in `study/data/interview-coding.csv`. The record may contain a verbatim participant phrase or an accurate paraphrase, but it must be grounded in the recording. Do not create a row for an unverified or missing session; mark the missing evidence in the analysis input instead.

1. What was easiest to do in the FIFA flow?
2. What caused uncertainty in the FIFA flow?
3. What information, if any, felt missing from the FIFA status view?
4. What felt unnecessary or distracting in the FIFA flow?
5. Did the FIFA status explain what would happen next? What made that clear or unclear?
6. Which FIFA wording felt unclear?
7. Before and after the partner transition, did you know where you were? Why or why not?
8. What was easiest to do in the Chess review flow?
9. What caused uncertainty in the Chess flow?
10. Did the explanation help you understand the mistake? What did you understand it to mean?
11. Which Chess wording or move notation felt unclear?
12. Did the practice activity feel related to the review? What made it feel related or unrelated?
13. What information or control felt unnecessary?
14. What would you change first in either flow?
15. Is there anything else you want the team to know about using the prototype?

Record participant wording or an accurate paraphrase in the interview-coding CSV with the anonymized participant ID, timestamp, product/theme, observation, and design implication. Never create a quote that was not spoken. The analysis gate treats one or more timestamped feedback rows per verified participant as the minimum interview/feedback completeness check.
