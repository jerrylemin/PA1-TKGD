# Group10 PA4 demo script

Target length: 15–20 minutes. All prototype records are fictional demo data. Do not present the prepared study templates as participant findings.

## 1. Continuity · 45 seconds

“PA1 compared FIFA.com’s browse-first ecosystem with Chess.com’s action-first learning surface. PA2 identified ticket-status confidence, handoff trust, beginner analysis entry, and practice continuity as high-value problems. For this PA4 correction, the task-authoritative directions are FIFA Alt 1 Status Dashboard and Chess Alt 2 Card Review Mode. PA3 historical records remain unchanged; this demo describes the corrected PA4 implementation.”

## 2. FIFA problem · 60 seconds

Show the PA3 continuity note or FIFA sketch. Explain that users first need an account-level answer—how many tickets are Confirmed, Pending, need action, or are cancelled—then a self-contained event card that says what is true, who owns the next step, and what they can do.

## 3. FIFA PA3 evidence · 60 seconds

Describe the Status Dashboard rationale without inventing participant findings: a status-first summary reduces ambiguity, while action-adjacent cards keep state, meaning, source, freshness, and ownership together. Point to the PA3 direction definition and `F-IMP-01/02/03/04/08` where applicable.

## 4. FIFA hi-fi demo · 4 minutes

1. Open `?mode=presenter#fifa` and point to the four account-level status cards before the event list.
2. Filter by Confirmed or Pending, then return to All and show that both events remain independently readable.
3. On the Pending Mexico card, read the meaning, FIFA ownership, next step, official source, freshness, and “No action needed.”
4. Open View Order, where the compact order history is secondary detail rather than the primary page structure.
5. On the Confirmed Toronto card, open View Tickets and save the eligible event to the calendar; contrast the disabled Pending control.
6. Choose Transfer Tickets and pause at “Before you leave FIFA.com.”
7. Read the partner name/domain and preserved context aloud.
8. Continue to the offline partner-boundary state, then return to the unchanged dashboard context.
9. Preview the unavailable state and retry to demonstrate recovery.

## 5. Chess problem · 60 seconds

Explain that a forced tutorial sequence creates entry-choice and pacing problems. Card Review Mode lets users scan performance categories, choose a personally relevant moment, inspect it in plain language, and opt into experimentation or practice.

## 6. Chess PA3 direction · 60 seconds

Show the Chess Alt 2 sketch or continuity note. Call out the required structure: dashboard summary chips, four visible selectable moment cards, no preselected card, a stable board as supporting context, and an optional bridge from explanation to safer-move trial and related practice. Do not attribute uncollected PA4 participant results.

## 7. Chess hi-fi demo · 4–5 minutes

1. Open `?mode=presenter#chess`; scan the four summary chips and four key-moment cards before selecting anything.
2. Select a non-queen card first and return, demonstrating independent self-selection rather than a prescribed first step.
3. Select Queen safety and identify `12. Qh5` and the `...Nxh5` consequence.
4. Reveal `Qe2` only after opening the safer-move explanation.
5. Enter the optional trial, make an incorrect destination attempt to show recoverable feedback, then select `d1` and `e2`.
6. Return to the selected card or dashboard without being forced into practice.
7. If demonstrating practice, open “Practice this idea,” try a wrong square, then complete `d1` to `d3` (`Qd3`).
8. Return to the same card dashboard; there is no global wizard or forced completion sequence.

## 8. Summative study design · 2 minutes

Show `study/study-plan.md`. Explain the four tasks per product, 2/1/0 success scale, timing/error/wrong-path/assistance measures, custom 1–5 questionnaire, neutral interview, counterbalanced product order, and recording convention.

## 9. Findings gate · 30 seconds

Say: “The summative result sections are intentionally incomplete. We have not supplied real participant sessions, recordings, questionnaire responses, task timings, or interview evidence yet. The package contains the collection and analysis mechanism and marks those requirements BLOCKED EXTERNALLY.”

## 10. Closing design implications · 60 seconds

FIFA’s implication is to keep account status, event meaning, source, ownership, action, and handoff boundary together. Chess’s implication is to make review user-selected while keeping explanation, optional safe experimentation, and relevant practice connected. The next local action is to run the prepared study, add verified consented evidence, rerun analysis, and replace the YouTube gate with a genuine demo URL.
