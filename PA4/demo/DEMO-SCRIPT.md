# Group10 PA4 demo script

Target length: 15–20 minutes. All prototype records are fictional demo data. Do not present the prepared study templates as participant findings.

## 1. Continuity · 45 seconds

“PA1 compared FIFA.com’s browse-first ecosystem with Chess.com’s action-first learning surface. PA2 identified ticket status confidence, handoff trust, beginner analysis entry, and practice continuity as the high-value problems. PA3 tested three alternatives per scenario with five independent AI reviewers and selected FIFA Alt 1 Status Dashboard and Chess Alt 1 Beginner Review Flow. PA4 turns those selected directions into testable hi-fi flows.”

## 2. FIFA problem · 60 seconds

Show the PA3 continuity note or the FIFA screenshot. Explain that the user needs a status-first answer: what is true now, who owns the next step, whether action is needed, and what happens before leaving FIFA context.

## 3. FIFA PA3 evidence · 60 seconds

Mention the PA3 Alt 1 result: 31/50 Independent Success, 11/50 Success With Hesitation, 8/50 Failure. Call out the documented gaps: Pending meaning, owner/timing, action adjacency, source/freshness, and partner boundary. Point to `F-IMP-01/02/03/04/08`.

## 4. FIFA hi-fi demo · 4 minutes

1. Presenter: open the FIFA flow at `?mode=presenter#fifa` and point to the status-first hierarchy.
2. Select the Pending event and open “What does Pending mean?”
3. Point to owner, expected timing, official source, last updated, and “No action needed.”
4. Open View Order and View Tickets to show the distinct detail routes.
5. Select the confirmed Toronto event and save it to the calendar.
6. Choose Transfer Tickets. Pause at “Before you leave FIFA.com.”
7. Read the partner name/domain and the preserved context aloud.
8. Continue to the offline partner boundary state, then return to the dashboard.
9. Use “Preview unavailable state” and retry to demonstrate recovery.

## 5. Chess problem · 60 seconds

Explain that PA2 and PA3 found recall demand and vocabulary load at analysis entry, while a good learning path needs to connect one mistake to an explanation and relevant practice.

## 6. Chess PA3 evidence · 60 seconds

Mention the PA3 Alt 1 result: 36/45 Independent Success, 8/45 Success With Hesitation, 1/45 Failure. Call out the documented gaps: no explicit start cue, notation translation, try-result feedback, beginner help, and persistent practice. Point to `C-IMP-01/02/03/04/08`.

## 7. Chess hi-fi demo · 4–5 minutes

1. Presenter: open Chess at `?mode=presenter#chess` and show the “Start Beginner Review” entry state.
2. Start the review and identify the single learning moment, `12. Qh5`.
3. Explain the plain-language cause: the queen moves onto a square attacked by the black knight on f6.
4. Reveal `Qe2` only after the consequence is understood, and show the board update and explanation.
5. Try the incorrect `Qh5` route to show recoverable feedback, then select the source and destination for `Qe2`.
6. Continue to “Practice this idea,” make the source-to-destination move `Qd3`, and complete it.
7. Finish the review or return to the review context.
8. Open the glossary or optional Full Analysis to show progressive disclosure.

## 8. Summative study design · 2 minutes

Show `study/study-plan.md`. Explain the four tasks per product, 2/1/0 success scale, timing/error/wrong-path/assistance measures, custom 1–5 questionnaire, neutral interview, counterbalanced product order, and recording convention.

## 9. Findings gate · 30 seconds

Say: “The summative result sections are intentionally incomplete. We have not supplied real participant sessions, recordings, questionnaire responses, task timings, or interview evidence yet. The package contains the collection and analysis mechanism and marks those requirements BLOCKED EXTERNALLY.”

## 10. Closing design implications · 60 seconds

FIFA’s implication is to keep status, source, ownership, action, and handoff boundary together. Chess’s implication is to keep beginner explanation, safe experimentation, and relevant practice in one guided path. The next local action is to run the prepared study, add verified evidence, rerun analysis, and replace the YouTube gate with a genuine demo URL.
