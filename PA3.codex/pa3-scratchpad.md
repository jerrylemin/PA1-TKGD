## A. Official PA3 brief

- PA3 is 150/500 points and has a two-week deadline (brief p.1).
- Requirement 1 (45%): at least two main scenarios and at least three different paper prototypes per problem; every prototype requires a YouTube demonstration; each must explain the new solution, motivation, problem/how solved, and team reflections on strengths and weaknesses; drawings should use colored pens on clean unlined paper (pp.2-3).
- Requirement 2 (45%): a comprehensive formative plan covering objectives, target users, method, success criteria and evaluated tasks/scenarios/functions; several sessions with 2-3 participants who have no prior prototype knowledge; systematically document qualitative observations, feedback, issues and improvements; choose the best lo-fi prototype after analysis (p.2).
- Peer Review (5%): required by the brief but intentionally excluded by the user's higher-priority scope (pp.2-3).
- Weekly Report (5%): track member tasks using the supplied template (p.3). The brief calls it Requirement 6 in body text but Requirement 4 in grading/deliverables.

- Formative Testing required sections: testing plan, testing result, evaluation, points of improvement, and the YouTube link for the best improved prototype (p.3).
